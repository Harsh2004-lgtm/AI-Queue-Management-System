About AI Queue Management System

A smart queue management system designed to reduce waiting time and make customer service more efficient.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make traditional waiting lines smarter, faster and more convenient for customers and staff.

How It Works

Customers can join the queue, receive a token, track their queue position and see the estimated waiting time without standing in a physical line.

For Staff

Staff members can manage customers from the admin dashboard, call the next customer and mark completed services.
